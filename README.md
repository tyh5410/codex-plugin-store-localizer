## 🔍 How It Works (Simple Explanation)

The localizer acts as a friendly translator between you and your plugin store. It watches for the store's language files and replaces them with your chosen language — all stored safely on your own computer. Because it uses a clever method that doesn't touch the original app, nothing gets broken, and you can switch back anytime.

## 🛠 Troubleshooting & Tips

### The app won't open on Windows

If double-clicking does nothing, try these:
1. Right-click the file and select "Open With" → "Java" (if installed) or "Run As Administrator."
2. Make sure you've extracted any downloaded `.zip` file completely before running.

### My plugin store still shows English

1. Close and reopen your Codex or ChatGPT app completely.
2. Re-run the localizer and click "Apply" again.
3. Check that you selected the correct app (Codex vs. ChatGPT) in the localizer's dropdown.

### How do I switch back to English?

Just open the localizer again, select "English," and click "Apply." Your store returns to its original state immediately.

### Is this safe to use?

Yes! The localizer never modifies your original app files. It creates a separate cache of translated text, so your system stays clean and reversible.
